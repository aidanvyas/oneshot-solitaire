# ONE-PASS SOLITAIRE: OFFICIAL RULEBOOK

## INTRODUCTION
One-Pass Solitaire is a challenging variant of Klondike Solitaire that tests your strategic planning and card management skills. With only one pass through the deck and four special storage spaces, every decision matters in this game of patience and foresight.

## COMPONENTS
- Standard 52-card deck
- 4 storage spaces
- 7 tableau piles
- 4 foundation piles

## SETUP
1. **Create the Tableau**: Deal 7 columns of cards as follows:
   - Column 1: 1 card
   - Column 2: 2 cards
   - Column 3: 3 cards
   - Column 4: 4 cards
   - Column 5: 5 cards
   - Column 6: 6 cards
   - Column 7: 7 cards

2. Only the top card of each column is face-up; all others remain face-down.

3. The remaining cards form the **Stock Pile**, placed face-down.

4. The 4 **Storage Spaces** begin empty.

5. The 4 **Foundation Piles** begin empty.

## OBJECTIVE
Move all 52 cards to the four foundation piles, with each foundation containing one suit arranged from Ace (low) to King (high).

## GAMEPLAY

### Drawing Cards
- Cards are drawn one at a time from the Stock Pile.
- Once a card is drawn, it must be played to the tableau, foundations, or storage; or left in the waste (discard area).
- You may only go through the Stock Pile **once**.

### Legal Moves

#### Tableau Rules:
- Cards in the tableau are built in descending order (King to Ace) with alternating colors (red and black).
- Example: On a black Queen, you may place a red Jack.
- Complete or partial sequences of cards may be moved together if they maintain proper sequence.
- Only a King (or a sequence headed by a King) may be placed on an empty tableau space.
- When a face-down card is exposed, it is turned face-up.

#### Foundation Rules:
- Foundations are built upward by suit, starting with Ace and ending with King.
- Example: Foundation starting with ♠A can only accept ♠2, then ♠3, and so on.
- Moving cards to foundations is optional, not mandatory.

#### Storage Spaces:
- Each of the 4 storage spaces can hold exactly one card.
- Cards in storage can be moved to the tableau (if a legal move) or to foundations.
- Cards can be moved between storage spaces, though there is rarely strategic value in doing so.
- Any card may be placed in storage, regardless of value or suit.

## RESTRICTIONS
- **No Undo**: Once a move is made, it cannot be reversed.
- **One Pass Only**: You may only go through the Stock Pile once.

## WINNING AND LOSING
- You win when all 52 cards have been transferred to the foundation piles in proper order.
- The game has no explicit losing condition, but it becomes impossible to win if no further legal moves exist.

## STRATEGY TIPS
1. Use storage spaces wisely; they are limited but powerful resources.
2. Consider keeping storage spaces open for crucial cards like Aces and Twos in the early game.
3. Prioritize exposing face-down cards in the tableau whenever possible.
4. Think several moves ahead, especially when deciding whether to move cards to foundations.
5. Remember that once a card is committed to a foundation, it cannot be returned to play.

## DIFFERENCES FROM TRADITIONAL KLONDIKE
- One pass through the Stock Pile only (versus three or unlimited passes).
- Four single-card storage spaces (not present in traditional Klondike).
- No undo function (many digital versions of Klondike allow this).
